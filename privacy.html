Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Information We Collect

NexByte Monitor records limited information when you use certain features:

  • The time of a download click. No visitor name is requested or stored for download clicks.
  • Your star rating, comment, and submission time when you send feedback. The feedback form does not ask for your name.
  • Technical connection data required to run a speed test, such as response time and download and upload measurements.

Comments are optional to identify you, so please avoid including personal or sensitive information.

How We Use Information

Anonymous click times help us understand use of the download link. Ratings and comments help us evaluate and improve NexByte Monitor. Speed test data is displayed to you as a measurement of your connection.

Storage and Retention

Anonymous download click records and feedback are temporarily stored through Google Apps Script under the service administrator's account. Click records are limited to 1,000 entries and feedback to 300 entries. Data is removed after 30 days and may be deleted sooner when these limits are reached. Names in older click records are removed when the updated script next processes or reads those records.

Third-Party Services

The speed test uses Cloudflare. To provide the measurement, Cloudflare may process your IP address and technical connection data. Cloudflare states that test results are collected for aggregated analysis of internet connection quality. When you open the download link, you are redirected to GitHub and are subject to GitHub's privacy policy.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement

Deletion Requests and Questions

To request deletion of your click records or feedback, or to ask questions about this policy, contact zenkscammer@gmail.com.
